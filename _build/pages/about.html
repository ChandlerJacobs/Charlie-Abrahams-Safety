About Melissa

Environment, Health & Safety professional known to colleagues as "Charlie". I started out drafting Safety Files and auditing construction sites in Klerksdorp in 2007 and now lead HSE management systems across multi-site mining operations.

Profile

Safe, compliant workplaces that people actually follow

My focus is on fostering safe and compliant workplaces through strong performance management, people development and training. I've worked on construction projects in Gauteng, the Western Cape, the Eastern Cape and Mpumalanga, at manufacturing plants, and across mining operations in Southern and Northern Africa.

That breadth means I can work comfortably under both the Occupational Health and Safety Act and the Mine Health and Safety Act, and I understand how a Safety File is used on the ground, not just how it looks on a shelf.
