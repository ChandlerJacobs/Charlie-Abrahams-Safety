The process

Where AI helps, and where it doesn't

AI speeds up capture, drafting and analysis. Legal judgements, final risk ratings and sign-off stay with a competent person.

  1. Capture

    Site photos, voice notes, inspection checklists and incident reports collected on a tablet.

  2. AI drafts

    Structured HIRA entries, incident summaries, registers and first-pass charts.

  3. AI checks

    Flags missing controls, expired certificates and layout conflicts on drawings.

  4. Competent-person review

    Each hazard, rating, control and legal reference verified and corrected.

  5. Sign-off & issue

    Version-controlled, signed and added to the live Safety File.
