How it works

What a consultation looks like

  1. Site walk & scope

    On-site visit to understand activities, hazards, contractors and timelines.

  2. Legal register & gap analysis

    Map what applies (OHS Act, Construction Regs, MHSA, ISO) and where you stand today.

  3. Documentation

    Safety File, HIRA, plans and procedures, drafted with AI assistance for speed.

  4. Review & sign-off

    Every rating, control and legal reference verified by me before issue.

  5. Audit & improve

    Monthly audits, trend reporting and corrective action follow-up.
