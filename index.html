What I do

Consulting services

Practical, legally referenced OHS work, from a single task risk assessment to a full integrated management system.

Safety File compilation

Complete Safety Files aligned to the Construction Regulations, 2014: H&S plans, legal appointments, registers and method statements, kept audit-ready.

HIRA & risk assessments

Baseline, issue-based and continuous risk assessments, job risk assessments, permits to work and safe work procedures.

Fall protection planning

Fall Protection Plans and worksite assessments for high-risk work at height, including rescue planning and clearance calculations.

ISO audits & IMS

Internal audits and gap analyses as an IRCA-certified Lead Auditor across ISO 45001, ISO 14001 and ISO 9001 integrated systems.

Incident investigation

Root cause analysis, corrective action tracking and LTI reporting that turns incidents into lasting fixes.

Training & contractor management

Inductions, toolbox talks, safety committee facilitation and monthly subcontractor Safety File audits.

New: AI-assisted documentation

Faster paperwork. The same competent-person sign-off.

I use AI to turn site photos, inspection checklists and incident notes into first-draft risk registers, trend charts and site drawings. Every hazard rating, control and legal reference is then checked and signed by me before it enters a Safety File.

The result is up-to-date analytics every month instead of every quarter, and more time on site where safety actually happens.
